📘 Learn Dynamics

Force

Force is a push or pull. SI Unit = Newton (N)

Newton's First Law

A body remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by an external force.

Newton's Second Law

Force = Mass × Acceleration F = ma

Newton's Third Law

Every action has an equal and opposite reaction.

Friction

Friction opposes motion between two surfaces.
