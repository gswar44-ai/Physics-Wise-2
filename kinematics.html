📘 Learn Kinematics

Distance

Distance is the total path travelled by an object. SI Unit: metre (m)

Displacement

Shortest distance between initial and final position. It has direction.

Speed

Speed = Distance / Time Unit = m/s

Velocity

Velocity = Displacement / Time Velocity has direction.

Acceleration

Acceleration = Change in Velocity / Time Unit = m/s²
