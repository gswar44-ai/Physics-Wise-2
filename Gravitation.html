📘 Learn Gravitation

Gravity

Gravity is the force by which Earth attracts objects towards its center.

Universal Law of Gravitation

Every object attracts every other object in the universe.

Mass

Mass is the amount of matter in a body. SI Unit = kilogram (kg)

Weight

Weight = Mass × Gravity W = mg

Free Fall

When an object falls only under gravity, it is called free fall.
